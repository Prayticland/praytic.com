Prayticland

A family of small private apps. Every app opens through its Telegram bot and signs in with Telegram, so there are no passwords to remember.

Stamps

A stamp collection, digitized page by page into a searchable catalogue.

@PrayticStampsBot

Gallery

A private collection of artworks, viewable in the browser and in Telegram.

@PrayticGalleryBot

Lifegame

A life management game for two players, with shared goals and progress.

@PrayticLifegameBot

USCIS Case Watcher

Immigration case tracking with status history and a progress comparison.

@PrayticImmigrationBot

Networker

A job posting scout that collects vacancies and scores them against a profile.

@PrayticVacanciesBot

Otgymanya

A gym routine tracker with an exercise catalogue and workout history.

@PrayticGymBot

Homunculus

An icon catalogue for the Prayticland apps.
